Tap to Save
Online guide · iPhone edition

Build an NFC contact card people can save with one tap

You will make a contact file, put it online, and write its link to an NFC sticker. When someone taps the sticker, your contact opens and they tap Create New Contact.

  • Time: about 20 minutes
  • Cost: one NFC sticker
  • Level: beginner

Before you start

  • An iPhone XS or newer
  • A blank NFC sticker (NTAG213 or NTAG215)
  • The free NFC Tools app from the App Store
  • A free GitHub account to host your contact file
Think before you shareAnyone who taps your tag can see everything in the contact file. Only include what you would put on a business card.

STEP 1Make the contact file

A contact file ends in .vcf. Every phone knows how to open one.

  1. Open Contacts and tap My Card at the top.
  2. Tap Share Contact, then Save to Files.
  3. Rename the file to contact.vcf.

STEP 2Put it online

The tag needs a web address that opens the file directly. GitHub Pages is free and works well for this.

  1. Sign in at github.com and create a new Public repository named yourusername.github.io.
  2. Click Add file → Upload files, add contact.vcf, then click Commit changes.
  3. Wait a minute or two. Your link is https://yourusername.github.io/contact.vcf
  4. Open that link in Safari to check that your contact card appears.

STEP 3Write the link to the tag

  1. Open NFC Tools and tap Write.
  2. Tap Add a record → URL / URI.
  3. Paste your link from Step 2 and tap ✓.
  4. Tap Write, then hold the top edge of your iPhone against the sticker until it confirms.
Metal blocks NFC

Stickers on metal surfaces won't read. Use anti-metal tags there.

Locking is permanent

If you lock a tag in NFC Tools, it can never be rewritten.

STEP 4Test it

  1. Close NFC Tools and unlock your iPhone.
  2. Hold the top of the phone near the sticker and tap the notification.
  3. Tap Create New Contact to confirm it saves.
It works whena friend's phone taps the tag and saves your contact in one tap.
TipTo update your info later, upload a new contact.vcf. The tag keeps working because it only stores the link.

Troubleshooting

ProblemFix
Nothing happens on tapUnlock the phone, close NFC Tools, and hold the top edge to the tag.
Link opens a page instead of the cardUse a direct file link like GitHub Pages, not Google Drive or iCloud.
"Write failed" in NFC ToolsMove away from metal and hold still for two seconds.
